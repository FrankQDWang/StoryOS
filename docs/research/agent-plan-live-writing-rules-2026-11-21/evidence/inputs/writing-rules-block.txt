Default rules for prose. Use these rules when you write or change prose of the novel. They are defaults for the style of the prose. When the author request, the Project instruction, or the instructions of a Skill ask for a different style, follow that and not the default. When the author request or the Project instruction gives a sample passage, write in the voice of that passage: its sentence length, its word choice, and its distance from the character. When a sample uses a device that a rule names, follow the sample. The Chinese phrases below are examples of a habit, and they are not forbidden words: one use that does work in the scene is correct, and the fault is the habit.

1. State the fact directly. Use a contrast only when the scene corrects a belief that a character held. Habit: 这不是结束，而是开始。
2. End a scene on its last event or line, inside what the viewpoint character knows. Do not state the meaning of the scene, and do not tell the future. Habit: 命运的齿轮开始转动；他不知道的是……
3. Show a feeling one time, through a choice, a spoken line, or a cost. You can name a small feeling plainly. Do not show a feeling and then explain it. Habit: 心中涌起一股暖流；他终于明白……
4. Give a physical reaction only when it belongs to this character at this moment. Habit: 眼中闪过一丝……；嘴角勾起一抹弧度。
5. Use a word of degree or doubt only when speed, force, or uncertainty changes what the reader understands. Habit: 微微、缓缓 on one action after another; 仿佛 where the character is sure.
6. Use a figure of speech only when it comes from this world and from the experience of this character. If not, give the literal detail. Habit: 如同石子投入湖面，泛起层层涟漪。
7. Let the content set the count and the length: one exact item and not three parallel items, sentences and paragraphs that change with the pace, and a cut between paragraphs and not a connective word. Habit: 三连排比；段首的“然而”“与此同时”。
8. Give each speaker their own words and their own aim. A line can be incomplete, evasive, or off the point. Habit: dialogue that explains like a manual.
9. Let a conflict cost something, and let it stay open across scenes. Describe only what moves the scene. Habit: a conflict that starts and ends in one paragraph.
10. Keep the dash and the paragraph of one sentence for a real interruption or a real stress. Habit: a dash in each paragraph.

When you find one of these habits in your text, write the event again in a different way; do not put a synonym in the place of the phrase.
