# Key of the calibration sample

The sample is the original novel 《临水茶馆》 in 10 Chapters. This key lists what a later Chapter needs and what it does not need. Chapter numbers are the order of the files.

## Characters (13)

| ID | Name | Fact that a later Chapter needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 沈知遥 | 28 years old. A designer in Shanghai. She comes back to the river town 青溪镇 for the funeral of her grandfather and stays to run his teahouse. |
| P2 | 沈怀山 | The grandfather. He ran 临水茶馆 for more than forty years and died in spring (Chapter 1). |
| P3 | 沈明川 | The father of 知遥. He lives in 杭州 and wants to sell the teahouse. |

| P7 | 陆小满 | 16, the younger sister of 陆砚, a student of the town middle school. |
| P8 | 周晚晴 | The childhood friend of 知遥, a teacher of the town primary school. |

## Foreshadowing lines that a later Chapter uses (4)

| ID | Planted | Used | Line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Chapter 2 | Chapter 8 | A locked camphor box under the counter, with no key. In Chapter 8 赵德明 brings the key; the box has the letters and the receipt that show that 顾青禾 paid half of the house. |
| F2 | Chapter 3 | Chapter 7 | The ledger has the entry "初九 寄二百" each month from 1991 to 2003. In Chapter 7 顾青禾 says that it was the tuition of her son, which 怀山 paid as her share of the profit. |
| F3 | Chapter 4 | Chapter 9 | 陆砚 always goes round by the new bridge and never steps on the old stone bridge. In Chapter 9 he tells that his father died in a flood while he repaired that bridge. |
| F4 | Chapter 5 | Chapter 10 | 陆砚 finds a crack in the main beam of the teahouse. In Chapter 10 a typhoon comes, and the beam that he reinforced holds. |

## Major events of each Chapter

| Chapter | Title | Major event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 归 | 知遥 comes back for the funeral, sees the closed teahouse, and meets 顾青禾. |
| 2 | 箱 | She sorts the things of the grandfather and finds the locked box. Her father says that he wants to sell and that he has signed nothing. |
| 3 | 账 | She reads the ledger. 程立 visits and offers 800,000 yuan for the house. |
| 4 | 椅 | She takes the broken chairs to 陆砚; he goes round by the new bridge. |
| 5 | 开 | She opens the teahouse for one trial week; the first guests come; 陆砚 finds the crack in the beam. |
| 6 | 报 | 周晚晴 brings an old town paper: the teahouse opened in 1979, not in 1983 as the sign says (1983 is the year of the purchase). 陆砚 reinforces the beam. |
| 7 | 约 | 知遥 finds that her father signed a letter of intent with 程立 two months ago; they quarrel. 顾青禾 explains the monthly 200 yuan. |
| 8 | 钥 | 赵德明 brings the key; the box shows that 顾青禾 owns half of the house. The sale cannot go on without her. |
| 9 | 桥 | 沈知南 comes from 北京; 陆砚 tells the story of his father and the bridge. |
| 10 | 风 | A typhoon; the beam holds; the town meeting keeps the riverside street; 知遥 decides to stay and tells 江屿 no. |

## The Conversation script (S7-D7 step 3, 4, and 5)

Three messages (step 3):
1. Request to remember: "请帮我记住：知遥的弟弟沈知南在北京读大学，学的是建筑。这个设定后面会一直用。"
2. Writing habit: "我写对话的时候从不用破折号表示停顿，停顿一律用省略号。你给我提修改建议时也照这个来。"
3. Intent that the manuscript does not have: "说一下后面的打算：顾青禾会在第十二章去世，她把自己那一半房产留给陆小满，正文现在还没写到。"

Two changed Chapters (step 4):
- Chapter 9 changed: 沈知南 now comes from 成都 and studies medicine. This conflicts with the entry of the author of message 1.
- Chapter 3 changed: 程立 offers 1,200,000 yuan, not 800,000.
- Chapter 10 does not change. It still says that 知南 studies architecture.
- Chapter 7 does not change. Its letter of intent still says 800,000 yuan.

Three questions in a new Project Conversation (step 5):
1. "茶馆当年买房子的钱是谁出的？"  Answer: 沈怀山 and 顾青禾, half each: she paid 2,100 of the 4,200 yuan (Chapter 8).
2. "沈知南现在在哪里读书，学什么？"  The Memory has the entry of the author (北京, architecture) with a conflict flag from the changed Chapter 9; the answer must ask the author.
3. "我之前说顾青禾后面会怎么样？"  Answer: she dies in Chapter 12 and leaves her half to 陆小满.
