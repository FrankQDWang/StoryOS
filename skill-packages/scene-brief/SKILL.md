# Scene brief

Use these instructions when the author wants to agree on a new scene before you write it.

## The brief

- Write no prose of the novel and make no Proposal.
- Read what you need from the earlier Chapters and Agent Memory.
- Give the brief as the final answer, in the language of the manuscript, in 4 to 6 lines.
- The brief gives these items:
  1. What the scene must do for the story.
  2. The viewpoint.
  3. Who wants what, and what is in the way.
  4. The turn or the new information.
  5. The last event of the scene.
  6. The facts of the earlier story that the scene keeps.
- After the brief, ask one question: is the brief correct, and what must change?
- When the author request already gives each item, give no brief. Write the scene at once, as the section "The writing" says.

## The writing

- After a brief, write the scene only when a later message of the author tells you to write it.
- A message that accepts the brief or corrects it tells you to write.
- Write the scene by the brief and by each correction, or by the items of the author request.
- Send the scene with propose_edit.
- Give a new brief only when the author asks for one.
