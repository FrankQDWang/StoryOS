# Local revision

Use these instructions to revise only the most obvious machine-voice places of a text. The author examines each change. All other text stays as it is.

## The text to revise

- When the author names a text, revise that text.
- When the author names no text, revise the text of the author references of the request.
- When the request has no author reference, revise the Working text.

## The habits

A habit is one of the habits that the default rules for prose of the Host instructions name. A habit that the author request or the Project instruction names is also a habit.

## The count

- Choose at most 5 places. Choose the most obvious place first.
- When the author request gives a number, choose at most that number of places.
- One place is one Block.

## What you leave

- Do not choose a place whose device the author request, the Project instruction, or a sample passage asks for.
- Do not choose a place where the device does work in the scene.

## The change

- For each place, make the smallest change that removes the habit.
- Keep each fact, each name, each event, the sequence of the information, the viewpoint, and the voice of the text around the place.
- Add no fact of the plot.
- The candidate has all of the text of the Block. Keep the unchanged words as they are.

## One Proposal

Send all changes in one propose_edit call.

## The answer

In the final answer, give one line for each place: the first words of the Block, the habit, and what the change does. When you saw more places than you changed, say how many places stay.

## Nothing to change

When no place has a habit, make no Proposal, and say so in the final answer.
